Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

koshercart ("the app") helps you compare kosher grocery prices at stores near you. We collect as little as possible — only what we need to run and improve the app. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information.

What we collect

  • Account — if you sign up, we store your email address and a securely-hashed password (handled by our sign-in provider, Supabase) so you can log in and sync.
  • Your lists, regulars, name, and settings — saved on your device, and when you are signed in, synced to your account so you can use them on another device.
  • Location — if you turn on "Use my location automatically," the app reads your device location on-device to pick the closest area. We store the area you use (e.g. "Teaneck"), not your precise coordinates.
  • Usage — basic in-app events such as app opens, the area you use, and what you search for. We use this to fix bugs, improve the app, and decide which stores and areas to add next.
  • Email updates — only if you opt in, we use your email to send occasional product updates and deals. You can unsubscribe any time in Settings or from any email.

What we do NOT do

  • We do not sell or rent your personal information.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • We do not run third-party advertising SDKs that profile you.

Store prices

Prices shown are gathered from participating stores' public websites and are provided for convenience. They may be out of date or contain errors — always confirm with the store.

Deleting your data

Use Account → Settings (gear) → Delete account to permanently remove your account, your synced lists and regulars, and everything stored on this device. Deleting the app removes the on-device copy.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the "last updated" date.
