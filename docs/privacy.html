koshercart — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

koshercart ("the app") helps you compare kosher grocery prices at stores near you. We built it to collect as little about you as possible. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information.

What we collect

  • Location — if you turn on "Use my location automatically," the app reads your device location to find nearby stores. Your location is used on your device to pick the closest area and is not sent to us or stored on any server.
  • Your lists, regulars, name, and settings — these are saved only on your device (local storage). We do not have accounts or a server that receives this data.

What we do NOT do

  • We do not require an account or a login.
  • We do not sell or share your personal information.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • We do not run third-party advertising SDKs that profile you.

Store prices

Prices shown are gathered from participating stores' public websites and are provided for convenience. They may be out of date or contain errors — always confirm with the store.

Deleting your data

Because your data lives only on your device, you can remove all of it at any time from Account → Settings (gear) → Delete account, or by deleting the app. This erases your lists, regulars, name, and preferences.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the "last updated" date.
