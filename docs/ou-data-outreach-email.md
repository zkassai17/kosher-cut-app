Hi,

My name is Zachary Kassai and I'm building **koshercart**, a free mobile app that helps kosher consumers compare grocery prices across their local stores so they can save money on the products they already buy.

I'm reaching out about the **OU Universal Kosher Database**. I saw that the OU has made a public certified-product database available, and I'd love to understand whether there's a way for a consumer app like mine to access it programmatically.

- To do that responsibly, I need a **reliable, authoritative way to confirm a given product is certified** before ever showing it to a user. My hard rule is that the app never guesses kosher status — so I want to rely on real certification data, not inference.

Could you help me understand:

1. Is there an **API, data feed, or bulk export** of OU-certified products (ideally with UPC/barcode) available to a consumer app, and under what terms?
2. If access is limited to certified companies, is there a **licensing or partnership path** for a consumer-facing product like this?
3. Are there **usage guidelines** I should follow for displaying OU certification status in an app?

I want to build this the right way and represent kosher certification accurately. Happy to hop on a call or share more about the app.
